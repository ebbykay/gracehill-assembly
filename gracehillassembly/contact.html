Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about visiting Gracehill Assembly

What time should I arrive for service?

We recommend arriving 15-20 minutes before service starts (8:30 AM on Sundays) to allow time for parking, finding a seat, and settling in before worship begins.

What should I wear?

Come as you are! You'll find people dressed in everything from casual wear to traditional attire and suits. The focus is on worshipping God, not what you wear.

Is there parking available?

Yes, we have ample parking space on our premises. Our parking team will guide you to available spots upon arrival.

Do you have programs for children?

Absolutely! We have a vibrant Children's Ministry with age-appropriate programs during our Sunday services. Our volunteers are trained to provide a safe, fun environment for kids to learn about God.

How can I become a member?

We offer membership classes periodically throughout the year. Fill out the contact form or speak to our welcome team after service to learn about the next membership class.

Is the church accessible for people with disabilities?

Yes, our facility is wheelchair accessible, and we have designated seating areas for those with mobility needs. Please let our ushers know how we can assist you.

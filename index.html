Our Core Values

The foundation of everything we do at Gracehill Assembly

The Word

We are committed to sound biblical teaching that transforms lives and builds strong foundations in Christ.

Prayer

Prayer is the heartbeat of our church. We believe in the power of prayer to change situations and lives.

Fellowship

We are a family, walking together in love, supporting one another, and building lasting relationships.

Service

We believe in serving our community and reaching out with the love of Christ in practical ways.

Community

Building a strong, supportive community where everyone belongs and grows together.

Outreach

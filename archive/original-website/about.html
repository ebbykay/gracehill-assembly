Welcome to Gracehill Assembly

Gracehill Assembly is more than a church—it is a family. We are a community of believers passionate about knowing Jesus and making Him known. Our heartbeat is to see lives transformed through the Word, Worship, and the Power of the Holy Spirit.

Through spirit-filled services, cell fellowships, evangelism and acts of love, we are building a generation of strong believers who influence the world for Christ.

Our Mission

To win souls, disciple believers, and equip men and women to walk in their God-given purpose, transforming communities through the power of Jesus Christ.

Our Vision

To raise Kingdom Giants—believers who will carry God's presence and transform nations through prayer, the Word, excellence, and love.

Our Core Values

Faith in Jesus | Love for People | Excellence in Service | Discipleship | Kingdom Advancement | Prayer & Worship | Evangelism

Our Journey

How God has led us through the years

2015

The Beginning

Gracehill Assembly was founded with a small group of believers meeting in a living room, united by a vision to raise Kingdom Giants.

2017

First Sanctuary

We moved into our first rented facility as the congregation grew to over 50 members.

2020

Growth & Expansion

Launched multiple ministries including Children, Youth, and Outreach programs.

2023

Our Permanent Home

Acquired our current facility at Broadcasting Junction, Weija, and established multiple cell groups.

2025

Year of Serving the Lord

Embracing our theme for the year with renewed commitment to serve God and our community.

What We Believe

Our foundational beliefs and core doctrines

The Bible

We believe the Bible is the inspired, infallible Word of God and our final authority for faith and practice.

Salvation

Salvation is through faith in Jesus Christ alone, by grace, not by works.

Baptism

We practice water baptism by immersion as an outward declaration of inward faith.

Holy Spirit

We believe in the baptism of the Holy Spirit and the operation of spiritual gifts.

Prayer

Prayer is essential for relationship with God and for seeing His power manifest.

Love & Service

We are called to love God and love others through practical service and outreach.

Welcome to Gracehill Assembly

Gracehill Assembly is more than a church—it is a family. We are a community of believers passionate about knowing Jesus and making Him known. Our heartbeat is to see lives transformed through the Word, Worship, and the Power of the Holy Spirit.

Through spirit-filled services, cell fellowships, evangelism, and acts of love, we are building a generation of strong believers who influence the world for Christ.

Founded on biblical principles and led by the Holy Spirit, we are committed to raising Kingdom Ambassadors who will transform nations and advance God's kingdom on earth.

Our Mission

To win souls, disciple believers, and equip men and women to walk in their God-given purpose, transforming communities through the power of Jesus Christ.

Our Vision

To raise Kingdom Giants—believers who will carry God's presence and transform nations through prayer, the Word, excellence, and love.

Our Core Values

Faith in Jesus | Love for People | Excellence in Service | Discipleship | Kingdom Advancement | Prayer & Worship | Evangelism

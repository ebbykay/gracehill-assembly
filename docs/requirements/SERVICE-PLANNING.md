# Service planning demonstration
The Programme Management demonstration includes a month calendar, programme selection, a fictional Sunday template, editable start time, participant assignments, integer activity durations, sequence controls, total duration and browser printing. Plans are held in memory per programme row and reset on reload. The template is illustrative and not an approved Gracehill order of service. Printing is explicitly labelled demonstration. No data goes to a server. Operational persistence, template approval, recurrence, assignments against actual identities, export to editable documents and access controls must be implemented separately. Browser tests cover template, addition, reordering, removal, calendar navigation and reset.

Activity editing and advisory overlap checks now compare scheduled time intervals of non-archived sample programmes with outlines. They do not establish actual venue or participant conflicts.
